# Stage 5A diagnostic design review

The user's request is to diagnose before detector changes and publish Stage 4. The supplied handoff proposes a diagnostic sequence; its proposed mechanisms are hypotheses, not established facts. Stage 4 was committed at `2ec21c4686c7dad7d60c177cb35fed2cd62d9280` and pushed to GitHub main. The Stage 4 completion receipt's `local_uncommitted_snapshot` field remains its truthful creation state. Its evolving documentation hashes resolve at that commit; old receipts are not rewritten.

The sequence is appropriate: image-only pose labeling, followed by unrestricted frozen-memory nearest-neighbor tracing and saved-map spatial analysis, then a combined decision. No detector parameters, inputs, thresholds, memory or inference logic will change. PCB2 is exposed development data now. Re-extracting the same frozen features for tracing is a diagnostic calculation, not a new model comparison.

Several claims need care. Stage 4 showed category-adapted detection after fitting new PCB2 normals, not zero-shot transfer or factory generalization. Observational pose association cannot establish a cause. Cross-location nearest neighbors are expected for an unrestricted detector and only become a useful failure hypothesis if misses differ meaningfully from hits after considering pose, mask size and context. Feature-grid cells describe overlapping receptive fields, not isolated physical patches. VisA masks are unions on multi-label images; a missing-labeled image's mask cannot always identify a uniquely missing component. Manual reference contexts may need an uncertain category.

The diagnostic protocol fixes top five unrestricted references, spatial radii 0.05/0.10/0.20 of normalized crop diagonal, a 4x4 normalized crop grid, and fixed PCB1 reference size bands. Outcome comparisons follow pose-label validation. Image-only labeling still has prior Stage 4 knowledge; it is not a retrospectively double-blind study. Label uncertainty and denominators are reported. Query-region score categories are operational descriptions rather than proof of representation failure. The two recipes remain primary/secondary history, not interchangeable candidates for a newly selected primary.

Stage 5A will recommend one controlled Stage 5B intervention only after the diagnostic evidence is reviewed. That intervention is not implemented here.
