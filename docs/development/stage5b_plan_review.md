2. **Rotate crop content before historical letterboxing.** Preserve historical crop detection and margins. Odd padding allocation can make a tensor rotation differ from crop rotation. The 195 controls require fresh modified-path inference: copying their historical outputs is not an invariance test.

3. **Restore map coordinates at the correct point.** Remove padding and use historical bilinear resizing to original crop dimensions, reverse that crop map, then paste at the historical source crop location.
