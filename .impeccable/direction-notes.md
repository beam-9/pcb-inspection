# Grounded directions

The product mechanism compares a real PCB with frozen normal-feature memory, while exposing why the research stopped. The audience reviews engineering evidence in a browser, with reports, benchmarks and interviews as its cultural home. Avoid the category-default metric dashboard and its predictable terminal-themed opposite.

Seven distinct candidates, ordered by resonance:
1. Scientific lab notebook: annotated photographic plates and a deliberate evidence trail.
2. Inspection workbench: a large image surface and task-oriented instrument controls.
3. Research-paper reprint: figure-first typography, captions and explicit dataset boundaries.
4. Engineering exhibition catalogue: oversized photographed PCB object, warm paper, editorial serif typography, rust-colored actions, and a practical inspection spread. Journey reads as an exhibit catalogue with evidence and decisions.
5. Technical drawing folio: geometric preprocessing diagrams and well-marked source views.
6. Newspaper investigation: dense but legible comparative findings and narrative transitions.
7. Precision instrument manual: measurement typography and explicit procedural states.

The Impeccable roll assigned candidate4. It is viable for the upload workspace, quiet model card, dense journey and mobile evidence browsing. No reroll is required.

Fused challengers considered on audience identification / product clarity only:
- Film cutting bench: comparisons become selected/archived experiment frames. Strong experimental sequence; weaker inspection affordances and source clarity than the catalogue.
- Cathode numeral stack: score values and frozen threshold are struck forward. Strong measurement character; ghost values and gauze introduce avoidable ambiguity around scientific scoring.
- Emigre bitmap specimen: native patch grids become pixel-honest editorial plates. Strong representational affinity; coarse bitmap typography is weaker for long scientific copy and fine defect imagery.
- Sleeve band, curved-crease shell and sleeping city remain in the reroll pool. Their metaphors add little to anomaly inspection and dataset boundaries.

Committed proposal: engineering exhibition catalogue. Real RGB/heatmaps are the artifacts; every scientific claim comes from frozen evidence. Fixed warm-paper canvas, ink text, rust actions, local Instrument Serif and DM Sans. No decorative technical grids, fake confidence, synthetic PCB evidence, or repeated icon-card scaffold.
